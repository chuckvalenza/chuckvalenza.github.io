Height Over Bore

HOB is the vertical distance from the center of your bore to the center of your optic. At close range, your bullet travels below your line of sight — this is why shots can hit low at short distances even with a correct zero. The closer you shoot, the bigger this effect.

MOA vs MRAD

1 MOA ≈ 1.047" at 100 yards (commonly rounded to 1"). 1 MRAD = 3.6" at 100 yards. Most American scopes use MOA; mil-spec and many precision scopes use MRAD. Never mix reticle and turret units without converting.

SFP vs FFP

SFP (Second Focal Plane): reticle subtensions are only accurate at one specific power — usually max magnification. Always verify which power your reticle is calibrated for. FFP (First Focal Plane): reticle subtensions are accurate at any magnification. No power correction needed.

Click Direction Convention

Most optics follow: elevation UP = counterclockwise, windage RIGHT = clockwise. However, some optics differ — always check the turret label. "Chase the bullet" is a helpful shortcut: if shots hit low-left, click up and right.
